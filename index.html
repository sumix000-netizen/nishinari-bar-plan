内見・許認可

  1. 仲介に平面図・住所、税込賃料、契約条件、夜間営業・酒類・カラオケの貸主承諾、残置物と原状回復範囲を確認。
  2. 職人同行で通電・容量、漏水、排水勾配、手洗い、換気、空調の年式・動作、上階への音、避難経路を確認。
  3. 契約・着工前に平面図と営業方法を持参し、南西部生活衛生監視事務所（06-4301-7240）、西成消防署（06-6653-0119）、必要に応じ西成警察署（06-6648-1234）へ相談。
  4. 飲食店営業の新規申請料は16,000円。食品衛生責任者と施設検査を手配。消防届は使用開始7日前まで。午前0時以降に酒類提供を主とする営業は10日前までの届出と用途地域・構造要件を確認。接待を伴う場合は風俗営業1号許可、深夜にカラオケを積極的に演出する場合は特定遊興への該当性を事前相談。
  5. 玉出は貸主の書面承諾、8席以上、工事110万円以内、開業資金約320万円以内が申込みの目安。軽改装・接待なしの場合、契約から開業まで4〜7週間を仮置き。

Viewings & Permits

  1. Ask the broker for the plan, address, rent including tax, lease terms, landlord approval for evening service, alcohol and karaoke, and terms for leftover fixtures and reinstatement.
  2. Take a tradesperson to check power and capacity, leaks, drain slope, handwash basin, ventilation, HVAC age and operation, noise to upper floors, and evacuation routes.
  3. Before signing the lease or starting work, take the floor plan and operating details to the Southwest Food Sanitation Office (06-4301-7240), Nishinari Fire Station (06-6653-0119) and, where applicable, Nishinari Police Station (06-6648-1234).
  4. The new restaurant permit fee is $101. Appoint a food sanitation supervisor and arrange the inspection. File the fire use notice seven days before use. For alcohol-led service after midnight, check the ten-day advance filing, zoning and layout rules. Confirm the Type 1 license if offering regulated guest entertainment, and ask about the rules for actively hosted late-night karaoke.
  5. For Tamade, use written landlord approval, at least eight seats, build-out at or below $6,918 and total startup capital around $20,126 or less as application criteria. Allow a provisional four to seven weeks from signing to opening for light renovations and no regulated guest entertainment.
